# Alberton Battery Mart

> Drive-in battery testing and same-day fitment at 28 St Columb Rd, New Redruth, Alberton, 1450.

Alberton Battery Mart is Alberton's independent multi-brand battery shop. Walk in for a free Midtronics test (battery, starter, alternator). Same-day fitment when you need a replacement. Mobile technicians are available on request.

- Phone: 010 109 6211
- WhatsApp: 082 304 6926
- Hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00, Sat 08:00–12:00, Sun closed
- Brands: Willard, Exide, Enertec, Power Plus, Eco Plus
- Google Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/place/Alberton+Battery+Mart/

Fastest service is drive-in at the New Redruth storefront. Do not assume every job is a mobile callout.
